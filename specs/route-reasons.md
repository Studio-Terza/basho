# route-reasons

Problem: there was no way to tell a deliberate detour from a bad route.

L1 Each walking leg carries a typed reason or an explicit "no reason recorded", never free text alone.
L2 A missing reason is visible on the leg card, without colour.
D1 Reason type: a kind such as point of interest, avoids a road, shorter, Bashō's path, or quieter, plus a typed link to the entry it refers to.
D2 Rendering on the leg card. after: D1
D3 Fill reasons only where the trip's own research notes (read-only) or existing trip data state one; mark everything else as no reason recorded. after: D1
Out: new routing, new content beyond stated reasons.
